12 year-end questions before you choose

Answer them alone or with your team. Use real numbers where you can. The answers that surprise you point to your business goals.

  1. What was the best decision we made in 2026?
  2. Which customers brought the most money and the least stress?
  3. What did we sell the most of, and what barely sold?
  4. Where did most new customers come from?
  5. What took the most time for the least money?
  6. Which task should the owner stop doing in 2027?
  7. What do customers complain about or ask for again and again?
  8. What would we do if we had twice the time?
  9. What would we stop if we had half the time?
  10. How much do we want to earn in 2027, and from how many customers?
  11. What one change would make next December calmer?
  12. If 2027 goes really well, what is different a year from now?

Plan your business year in 45 minutes

  1. 10 minutes: look back. Write three wins and three problems of 2026. Look at real numbers: revenue, customers, hours worked.
  2. 15 minutes: answer the questions. Go through the 12 questions below, alone or with your team. Short answers are fine.
  3. 10 minutes: pick three business goals. One for customers or money, one for the product or team, one for yourself as the owner.
  4. 10 minutes: make it visible. Turn your most important goal and its first step into a phone lock screen, print it for the office wall, or make a full board.

Questions

What is a small business vision board?

A board with the goals of your business for the coming year, such as more customers, higher prices, a first hire or weekends off. It keeps the year's priorities in front of you, so daily work does not push them aside.

How do you make a vision board for your business?

Look back at the year with real numbers, answer a few honest questions, pick three business goals and make them visible. This page has 36 business goals with a first step, 12 year-end review questions and a 45-minute plan.

What should I put on a business vision board?

Goals for customers, money, your product, your team, your marketing and yourself as the owner. Add one first step to each goal so the board says what to do this week, not only what to hope for.

Is a vision board useful for a business, not only personal goals?

Yes, if it is concrete. A number, a date and a first step turn a wish into a plan you see every time you unlock your phone.
